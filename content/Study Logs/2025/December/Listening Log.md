## The Method

As discussed in this video from [livakivi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4_q57-mixs) I intend to watch a single episode of an anime repeatedly until I feel that I can grasp the majority of what is being said. Others have recommended the same method to improve listening comprehension, and since that seems to be what I am weakest at, I thought I would give this a try. Normally it would be beneficial to find Japanese subtitles or even better a script of the episode, but for Cowboy Bebop at least I cannot find either. I will give the first episode probably about 10 rewatches over ten days and try to see what happens. Some people also suggest trying to transcribe what you hear, which will significantly slow the listening process. I may try to do so after the initial 10 watches and track compare how long the process takes versus the length of the episode. I am including full episode lengths, which includes intro and outro, although I am watching neither of those.
